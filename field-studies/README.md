# Natural Gas Field Studies

A ten-book research series directed by John Barreto. The books follow U.S. natural gas from wells and storage facilities to power plants, factory bills and Henry Hub futures. AI tools conducted the analyses, wrote the manuscripts and prepared the research companions under Barreto’s direction.

## A result you can check

On January 28, 2022, the front-month Henry Hub chart fell $1.626/MMBtu while the incoming March contract rose $0.356/MMBtu. The previous close for February was $6.265 and March was already trading at $4.283. On Friday, March settled at $4.639. Book 10 documents 57 opposite-direction moves in 235 verified handoffs. The companion includes the event-level data and Python analysis.
